LLM-Driven Mental Health Companion Robot

Undergraduate Research Assistant — MITHRIL Lab

Ingenuity Labs Research Institute, Queen's University · Supervised by Prof. Matthew Pan · May – Aug 2026

Designed, fabricated, and programmed a tendon-driven, continuum-stem animatronic flower robot from the ground up, serving as the physical embodiment for an LLM-driven mental health companion research platform in the lab's Human-Robot Interaction research program.

  • Mechanical Design: CAD in Fusion 360, laser-cut acrylic subframes, and 3D-printed structural parts, including a 4-tendon continuum stem with ball-and-socket joints.
  • Software Architecture: Built the full ROS 2 (Humble) host stack — a Tkinter control GUI with a live 3D kinematics preview, a forward-kinematics solver mapping bend targets to antagonistic servo commands, and a mux layer bridged to the robot over micro-ROS/Wi-Fi.
  • Embedded Systems: Developed the ESP32 firmware (PlatformIO/C++) driving 4 servos through a PCA9685 I2C controller, WS2812B petal LEDs, and an encoder-controlled base motor, with a self-healing Wi-Fi/micro-ROS reconnection protocol using mDNS discovery.
  • Perception: Implemented OpenCV-based perception nodes — HSV-segmentation ball localization and RetinaFace face tracking — to lay the groundwork for closed-loop and attention-based behaviors.
  • Documentation: Authored a 27-page technical handbook covering assembly, tuning, software architecture, and setup/operation for lab reproducibility and handoff.

Three-Link Motion Tracking Arm

Jan. 2026

Modular, 3D-printed ball-and-socket arm designed to track the real-time location of each link and end-effector.

  • Mechanical: Modular joint system with spring-loaded friction pads to maintain constant ball-and-socket friction.
  • Embedded Systems: Real-time tracking via ESP32-S3; switched from noisy MPU6050 + QMC magnetometers to an Adafruit LSM6DSOX + LIS3MDL 9DOF IMU for stable data.
  • Software: Embedded control in C++, Python-based HMI, ROS 2 for communication.

Ground-up build

Pneumatic Engine

Engineering 12, High School

Conceived, designed, and constructed a pneumatic engine, overcoming tight tolerances and complex 3D-printed geometries.

  • Engineered piston-crank geometry and valve timing to achieve linear-to-rotary motion transfer.

Dual Extruder Retrofit

Ender 3 Pro

Retrofitted a custom dual-extrusion print head assembly using a custom sheet-metal backplate and modified firmware for multi-material 3D printing.

  • Upgraded the mainboard for additional stepper drivers and a second hotend; configured custom Marlin firmware to manage dual extrusion.
  • Designed a custom sheet-metal backplate to mount dual hotends within the original gantry's constraints and produced drawings for external manufacturing.
  • Trade-off: multi-material/multi-color prints worked, but the added complexity reduced machine reliability -- an early lesson that shaped how I approach design trade-offs.
